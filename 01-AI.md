# 01 - Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## 📖 Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that focuses on building systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions, and learning from experience.

---

## 🧠 Explanation

### Traditional Programming vs AI (Machine Learning)

| | Traditional Programming | Machine Learning |
|---|---|---|
| You provide | Data + Rules | Data + Correct Answers |
| Computer produces | Answers | Rules (a trained model) |
| Example | `if marks >= 35: print("Pass")` | Train on thousands of labeled spam / not-spam emails |

In traditional programming, the developer writes the rules.
In machine learning, the system finds the rules from examples.

### How AI, ML and DL are related

```
Artificial Intelligence
└── Machine Learning
    └── Deep Learning
```

- **AI**: the overall goal of making machines behave intelligently. This includes older rule-based systems too (for example, a chess program with hand-written logic).
- **Machine Learning (ML)**: a way of achieving AI where the system learns patterns from data.
- **Deep Learning (DL)**: a type of ML that uses neural networks with many layers.

> ⚠️ AI and ML are **not the same thing**. ML is a subset of AI.

### Types of AI (by capability)

| Type | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow AI (ANI) | Does one specific task well | Exists today (all current AI) |
| General AI (AGI) | Can learn and perform any intellectual task like a human | Does not exist yet |
| Super AI (ASI) | Surpasses human intelligence | Hypothetical |

---

## ⭐ Why it is Important

- Automates repetitive and data-heavy tasks.
- Finds patterns in data that humans cannot easily see.
- Powers products used by billions of people daily.
- Is now a core skill area for software developers.

- Face unlock on smartphones

## 🎤 Interview Explanation

**Q: What is AI?**

> "AI is the field of building systems that can perform tasks that normally need human intelligence, like understanding language, recognizing images, or making decisions. Instead of programming every rule manually, modern AI systems, especially machine learning models, learn patterns from data. For example, a spam filter learns from labeled emails instead of me writing spam rules by hand."

**Q: What is the difference between AI and ML?**

> "AI is the broader goal of making machines intelligent. Machine learning is one approach to achieve it, where the system learns from data. So every ML system is AI, but not every AI system uses ML."

**Common mistakes to avoid in interviews**

- ❌ "AI and ML are the same."
- ❌ "AI thinks like a human brain." Current AI finds statistical patterns in data; it does not think or understand like a human.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

1. AI = machines performing tasks that normally need human intelligence.
2. Traditional programming: you write rules. ML: the system learns rules from data.
3. AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL.
4. All AI today is Narrow AI; AGI does not exist yet.
5. AI works on patterns in data, not human-like understanding.
